## 1. The Beautifully Imperfect World of Adam Elliot

**Thesis for intro:** tragic + funny + tender as one tone, not three modes; clay as the body of imperfect lives.

- Official: films are “Clayographies” — clay animated biographies of bittersweet lives of family and friends; seven films; 1000+ festivals, 130+ awards; every prop/set/character handmade; zero CGI or AI. ([adamelliot.au](https://www.adamelliot.au/))
- UniMelb: every script starts with fear and anger-as-injustice; two objectives — “make people laugh; make people cry.” ([VCA alumni](https://finearts-music.unimelb.edu.au/about-us/news/adam-elliot,-filmmaker-alumni-stories-at-the-vca,-university-of-melbourne))
- RogerEbert: kintsugi / embrace flaws; “warts and all”; aesthetic imperfectness mirrors psychological imperfectness; no-airbrushing rule. ([Living Forward](https://www.rogerebert.com/interviews/living-forward-adam-elliot-on-memoir-of-a-snail))
- BFI: “chunky wonky”; fingerprints on clay; story first, animation second — audience forgives bad animating, never a bad story. ([Sight & Sound](https://www.bfi.org.uk/sight-and-sound/interviews/audience-will-forgive-bad-animating-theyll-never-forgive-bad-story-adam-elliot-memoir-snail))
- ABC: “Without the dark, the light has no meaning”; tears made with hair gel + lubricant (comic fact that embodies the tone). ([ABC 2024](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2024-10-16/memoir-of-a-snail-adam-elliot-interview-claymation/104455884))

## 2. Clayographies: Stories of Ordinary, Extraordinary People

- Official definition (use his word Clayographies; contrast with Pixar/DreamWorks/Aardman family fare). ([adamelliot.au](https://www.adamelliot.au/), [ABC](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2024-10-16/memoir-of-a-snail-adam-elliot-interview-claymation/104455884))
- Based on real people: Mary and Max ≈ pen friend in New York with Asperger’s; anger at how he was treated. ([UniMelb](https://finearts-music.unimelb.edu.au/about-us/news/adam-elliot,-filmmaker-alumni-stories-at-the-vca,-university-of-melbourne))
- Memoir of a Snail: less purely factual than Mary and Max; composites — father’s mild hoarding / mother’s collecting; friend with cleft palate who became flamboyant; Elliot sees himself in Gilbert / Pinky. ([ABC](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2024-10-16/memoir-of-a-snail-adam-elliot-interview-claymation/104455884), [RogerEbert](https://www.rogerebert.com/interviews/living-forward-adam-elliot-on-memoir-of-a-snail))
- Real life > fiction; docs matter (Errol Morris; *My Beautiful Stutter*). ([BFI](https://www.bfi.org.uk/sight-and-sound/interviews/audience-will-forgive-bad-animating-theyll-never-forgive-bad-story-adam-elliot-memoir-snail))
- ScreenAnarchy 2015: characters as extensions of self; outsider / marginalized archetypes; “never let the truth get in the way of a good story.” ([ScreenAnarchy](https://screenanarchy.com/2015/07/interview-adam-elliot-on-being-an-animator-winning-an-oscar-and-feeling-like-an-outsider.html))
- Career plan: trilogy of trilogies (3 short / 3 long short / 3 features); goal of nine films. ([UniMelb](https://finearts-music.unimelb.edu.au/about-us/news/adam-elliot,-filmmaker-alumni-stories-at-the-vca,-university-of-melbourne))

## 4. Mary and Max / Memoir of a Snail (catalog FilmCards)

**Mary and Max (2009)**

- Pen-pal friendship; loneliness; Asperger’s; opening night Sundance (first animated + first Australian in that slot — cite carefully via secondary).
- Nick Park quote via ACMI: stop-motion directing “creative with a gun at your head”; Elliot had script memorized, characters “directed themselves.” ([ACMI](https://www.acmi.net.au/stories-and-ideas/interview-adam-elliot/))
- More factual of the two features. ([RogerEbert](https://www.rogerebert.com/interviews/living-forward-adam-elliot-on-memoir-of-a-snail))

**Memoir of a Snail (2024)**

- Origin: father’s death / sheds of stuff → fascination with hoarding + trauma; merged with cleft-palate friend notes; snails after Lady Bird killed “ladybird” draft; snail = introvert retracting into shell. ([ABC](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2024-10-16/memoir-of-a-snail-adam-elliot-interview-claymation/104455884), [BFI](https://www.bfi.org.uk/sight-and-sound/interviews/audience-will-forgive-bad-animating-theyll-never-forgive-bad-story-adam-elliot-memoir-snail))
- 16 drafts / ~8 years writing; shoot ~33 weeks; ~$4M budget. ([RogerEbert](https://www.rogerebert.com/interviews/living-forward-adam-elliot-on-memoir-of-a-snail), [FilmInt](https://filmint.nu/adam-elliot-memoir-of-a-snail-ciff-2024-interview-jonathan-monovich/))
- Themes: cages; Grace must become whole before Gilbert returns (not missing half). ([RogerEbert](https://www.rogerebert.com/interviews/living-forward-adam-elliot-on-memoir-of-a-snail))
- Pinky as Harold and Maude spirit / comic relief; Elliot identifies more with Pinky now. ([BFI](https://www.bfi.org.uk/sight-and-sound/interviews/audience-will-forgive-bad-animating-theyll-never-forgive-bad-story-adam-elliot-memoir-snail), [ABC](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2024-10-16/memoir-of-a-snail-adam-elliot-interview-claymation/104455884))
- Annecy Cristal (Elliot’s fifth). ([ABC](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2024-10-16/memoir-of-a-snail-adam-elliot-interview-claymation/104455884))
- Kierkegaard quote reused from Uncle; snails can’t reverse. ([RogerEbert](https://www.rogerebert.com/interviews/living-forward-adam-elliot-on-memoir-of-a-snail), [FilmInt](https://filmint.nu/adam-elliot-memoir-of-a-snail-ciff-2024-interview-jonathan-monovich/))

**Compare/contrast for section:** friendship across distance vs life as memoir; pen-friend fidelity vs composite “cages”; both celebrate imperfect people in imperfect clay.

## 5. What Makes an Adam Elliot Film Feel Like an Adam Elliot Film?

- **Narration:** began as low-budget necessity (fill gaps, avoid walking/lip-sync); now distilled with editor; can become overbearing if not pared. ([FilmInt](https://filmint.nu/adam-elliot-memoir-of-a-snail-ciff-2024-interview-jonathan-monovich/)); also SMH 2015: “narration is a wonderful time-saving device.”
- **Handmade look:** paper, clay, wire, paint; fire = cellophane; smoke = cotton wool; fingerprints kept; “made by human beings.” ([BFI](https://www.bfi.org.uk/sight-and-sound/interviews/audience-will-forgive-bad-animating-theyll-never-forgive-bad-story-adam-elliot-memoir-snail), [FilmInt](https://filmint.nu/adam-elliot-memoir-of-a-snail-ciff-2024-interview-jonathan-monovich/))
- **Loneliness / outsiders / disability / misunderstanding** as moral center (injustice). ([UniMelb](https://finearts-music.unimelb.edu.au/about-us/news/adam-elliot,-filmmaker-alumni-stories-at-the-vca,-university-of-melbourne))
- **Comedy-tragedy balance** as craft problem he always rewrites toward. ([RogerEbert](https://www.rogerebert.com/interviews/living-forward-adam-elliot-on-memoir-of-a-snail), [FilmInt](https://filmint.nu/adam-elliot-memoir-of-a-snail-ciff-2024-interview-jonathan-monovich/))
- **Physical imperfect characters** = theme made visible (chunky wonky, no airbrushing).
- Intergenerational / unlikely friendships (Mary–Max, Grace–Pinky). ([BFI](https://www.bfi.org.uk/sight-and-sound/interviews/audience-will-forgive-bad-animating-theyll-never-forgive-bad-story-adam-elliot-memoir-snail))

## 6. Where to Start

Draft recommendation options (pick one voice in final copy):

1. **Harvie Krumpet** — short, Oscar-winning, complete Clayography in ~23 minutes; free to stream; lowest commitment.
2. **Mary and Max** — if they want the fullest classic feature friendship story.
3. **Uncle → Cousin → Brother** — if they want to see the style born in miniature.

Avoid starting with Memoir of a Snail for first-timers who expect “cute clay” — denser trauma stack; better as second feature after Mary and Max or after Harvie.
